<div style="background:#f9fafb;border-radius:12px;padding:24px 28px;margin:24px 0;box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)">
  <p><strong>ISO/IEC 27701:2025</strong> is the refreshed privacy standard for building a <strong>Privacy Information Management System (PIMS)</strong>. The 2025 edition makes 27701 a <strong>stand-alone standard</strong> while keeping strong alignment with <strong>ISO/IEC 27001:2022</strong> and <strong>ISO/IEC 27002:2022</strong>.</p>

  <p>For Australian organisations, this means you can certify privacy on its own — or integrate it with your ISMS for a single, efficient governance system that buyers and regulators recognise.</p>

  <p>This page explains what changed, why security + privacy work best together, and how to reach <strong>dual assurance</strong> quickly using Microsoft 365 for evidence.</p>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#111827;font-size:1.6rem;font-weight:800;margin-top:2rem">1️⃣ What’s New in ISO/IEC 27701:2025</h2>

<div style="background:#f9fafb;border-radius:12px;padding:16px 20px;margin-top:12px">
  <ul style="margin:0 0 0 18px">
    <li><strong>Stand-alone PIMS:</strong> 27701 is no longer only an “extension” to 27001 — it can be implemented and certified independently.</li>
    <li><strong>Terminology &amp; control alignment:</strong> Updated language to match <strong>ISO/IEC 27001:2022</strong> and <strong>ISO/IEC 27002:2022</strong> for smoother dual operations.</li>
    <li><strong>Sharper governance focus:</strong> Clearer accountability for controller/processor roles, oversight, and metrics.</li>
    <li><strong>Interoperability:</strong> Continued mapping support for global privacy frameworks (e.g., ISO/IEC 29100) to ease cross-jurisdiction readiness.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<p style="margin-top:10px;color:#4b5563">
In practice: you can pursue privacy certification alone <em>or</em> run a combined audit with ISO 27001 for a unified management system and less duplicate evidence.
</p>

<h2 style="color:#111827;font-size:1.6rem;font-weight:800;margin-top:2rem">4️⃣ A Practical Roadmap to Dual Assurance</h2>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Weeks 1–2:</strong> Confirm scope (systems, data, processors), align ISMS/PIMS governance, draft updated privacy policy and roles.</li>
  <li><strong>Weeks 3–6:</strong> Build ROPA in SharePoint; stand up DPIA workflow (Power Automate + Teams Approvals); define rights handling SLAs.</li>
  <li><strong>Weeks 7–10:</strong> Evidence automation — monthly exports (access reviews, supplier logs), versioned artefacts, metrics dashboard.</li>
  <li><strong>Weeks 11–12:</strong> Readiness review and sampling rehearsal; close actions and book dual audit window.</li>
</ul>

<p style="margin-top:8px;color:#4b5563">Most organisations with a healthy ISMS can add 27701 in <strong>4–8 weeks</strong>; new dual programmes typically complete in <strong>12–16 weeks</strong> depending on scope and suppliers.</p>

<h2 style="color:#111827;font-size:1.6rem;font-weight:800;margin-top:2rem">5️⃣ Migrating from ISO/IEC 27701:2019</h2>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Gap-check your terms &amp; controls</strong> against the 2025 edition (aligned to 27001:2022/27002:2022).</li>
  <li><strong>Re-validate roles</strong> (controller/processor) and update related artefacts (ROPA rows, DPA clauses).</li>
  <li><strong>Unify governance</strong>: one management review, one audit calendar, one corrective-action register for ISMS + PIMS.</li>
  <li><strong>Refresh metrics</strong> for privacy performance and feed into ISMS performance evaluation.</li>
</ul>

<blockquote style="background:#eef2ff;border-left:4px solid #4f46e5;padding:12px 18px;border-radius:8px">
  💡 <strong>Tip:</strong> Keep your artefacts simple and repeatable (lists, workflows, exports). Automation beats catch-up every audit cycle.
</blockquote>
