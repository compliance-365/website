<div style="background:#f9fafb;border-radius:12px;padding:24px 28px;margin:24px 0;box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)">
  <p><strong>ISO 27001</strong> defines how organisations protect information through a structured <strong>Information Security Management System (ISMS)</strong>.  
  <strong>ISO 27701</strong> builds on that foundation with a <strong>Privacy Information Management System (PIMS)</strong> — extending your security program to cover personal data governance, privacy rights, and lawful processing.</p>

  <p>Together, they form a <strong>dual framework</strong> that demonstrates trust, transparency, and regulatory alignment — essential for healthcare, government, and SaaS vendors in Australia.</p>

  <p>This playbook explains when ISO 27001 alone is enough, when to extend to ISO 27701, and how to align both for a single, efficient certification path.</p>
</div>

<h2 style="color:#111827;font-size:1.6rem;font-weight:800;margin-top:2rem">2️⃣ When ISO 27001 Alone Is Enough</h2>

<ul>
  <li>You handle only internal data or limited business contact details.</li>
  <li>Clients request <strong>security assurance</strong> but not privacy governance.</li>
  <li>You’re early in maturity and focusing on foundational risk and control alignment.</li>
  <li>Regulatory exposure (e.g., health, EU, or government) is minimal.</li>
</ul>

<blockquote style="background:#ecfdf5;border-left:4px solid #10b981;padding:12px 18px;border-radius:8px">
  ✅ <strong>Outcome:</strong> ISO 27001 provides assurance that your systems are secure and your governance processes meet enterprise expectations.
</blockquote>

<hr style="margin:36px 0;border:0;border-top:1px solid #e5e7eb" />

<h2 style="color:#111827;font-size:1.6rem;font-weight:800;margin-top:2rem">3️⃣ When to Add ISO 27701</h2>

<p>Adopt ISO 27701 when your organisation:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Processes <strong>personal or sensitive information</strong> such as patient data or customer identifiers.</li>
  <li>Responds to government or health-sector RFPs requiring privacy assurance.</li>
  <li>Manages user data across multiple jurisdictions or cloud platforms.</li>
  <li>Faces privacy reviews, DPIA obligations, or vendor security questionnaires referencing ISO 27701.</li>
</ul>

<blockquote style="background:#fef3c7;border-left:4px solid #f59e0b;padding:12px 18px;border-radius:8px">
  🧭 <strong>Goal:</strong> Demonstrate that your organisation doesn’t just secure data — it governs how personal data is used, shared, and erased responsibly.
</blockquote>
