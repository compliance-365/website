AI transparency note

Where a tenant enables the optional AI assistant, it runs against that tenant's own Azure OpenAI resource, in their own tenant and subscription — no third-party service, and never Compliance365, receives compliance data through this feature. Only the specific register data a practitioner chooses to include with a given question is sent to the model; nothing is sent automatically. The assistant cannot access systems, modify records, or take any action — it only ever returns draft text for a human to review, and every response is labelled as a draft. It's instructed to say when it lacks enough information rather than invent details, and never to claim a certification or audit outcome has been achieved.
