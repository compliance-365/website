## Problem
The client, a rapidly scaling digital health provider, needed ISO 27001 certification to meet tender requirements and assure hospitals and government clients of data security. Their controls were informal, with fragmented policies and limited audit evidence.

## Approach
- Performed a full ISO 27001:2022 gap assessment covering Entra ID, Microsoft 365 E5, and AWS workloads  
- Established an ISMS with policies, risk register, and Statement of Applicability in SharePoint  
- Implemented evidence automation via Power Automate and Microsoft Defender compliance reports  
- Trained the internal security and IT teams for ongoing self-maintenance  

## Outcome
- Achieved ISO 27001 certification within 12 weeks  
- Streamlined annual surveillance audits with automated evidence capture  
- Improved procurement success rate with government and enterprise buyers  
- Enhanced executive visibility into risk posture and control maturity
