## Problem
The client faced enterprise procurement blockers without **ISO 27001 certification**. Controls existed but were informal, with fragmented policies and limited audit evidence.

## Approach
- Performed an **ISO 27001:2022** gap assessment across **Entra ID**, **M365 E5**, and **AWS** workloads  
- Built a right-sized **ISMS** (policies, risk register, **Statement of Applicability**) in SharePoint  
- Automated evidence via **Defender/M365 reports** and **Power Automate**  
- Trained internal teams to maintain the ISMS and evidence cadence

## Outcome
- **Certified in 12 weeks** with clean findings  
- Faster surveillance cycles using automated evidence  
- Improved win rate on government and enterprise tenders  
- Executive dashboards for risk and control maturity

## At a glance
- **Stack:** Microsoft 365 E5, Entra ID, AWS  
- **Controls:** MFA, logging, backup/DR, secure SDLC  
- **Deliverables:** ISMS pack, SoA, risk register, audit evidence plan
