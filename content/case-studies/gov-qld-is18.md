## Problem
A Queensland Government agency needed a clear, evidence-based approach to meet **IS18** reporting obligations. Documentation showed mixed maturity across **ISO 27001** and the **ASD Essential Eight**, making it hard to present a unified assurance position.

## Approach
- Mapped IS18 control requirements to **ISO 27001:2022 Annex A** and **Essential Eight** maturity levels  
- Ran stakeholder interviews and evidence reviews to baseline control maturity  
- Built a single **IS18 assessment report** with risk ratings and time-boxed remediation items  
- Deployed a **SharePoint dashboard** to track actions, owners, and evidence automatically

## Outcome
- A consolidated **IS18 report** combining ISO 27001 & Essential Eight results  
- Prioritised roadmap to reach **Maturity Level 3** in key areas  
- >50% reduction in annual reporting effort through evidence reuse and automation  
- Stronger executive visibility across governance, risk, and technical domains

## At a glance
- **Frameworks:** IS18, ISO 27001:2022, ASD Essential Eight  
- **Evidence:** M365 (SharePoint, Defender, Entra), automated exports  
- **Deliverables:** IS18 report, treatment plan, metrics dashboard
