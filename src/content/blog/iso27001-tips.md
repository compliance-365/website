<div style="background:#f9fafb;border-radius:12px;padding:22px 26px;margin:24px 0;box-shadow:0 2px 8px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)">
  <p><strong>ISO 27001</strong> can seem daunting, but it doesn’t need to start that way. Every successful Information Security Management System (ISMS) begins with a few disciplined, achievable steps that demonstrate progress and build internal confidence.</p>
  <p>These five quick wins help you prove maturity early, engage stakeholders, and prepare clean evidence for your eventual audit—without bureaucracy or burnout.</p>
</div>

<hr style="margin:36px 0;border:0;border-top:1px solid #e5e7eb" />

<h2 style="color:#111827;font-size:1.6rem;font-weight:800;margin-top:2rem">1️⃣ Define Your ISMS Scope Clearly</h2>

<ul>
  <li><strong>What it means:</strong> Decide exactly what’s <em>in</em> and <em>out</em> of scope—locations, systems, products, and people.</li>
  <li><strong>Why it matters:</strong> A clear boundary prevents endless debates and reassures auditors your ISMS is deliberate, not accidental.</li>
</ul>

<blockquote style="background:#eef2ff;border-left:4px solid #4f46e5;padding:12px 18px;border-radius:8px">
  💡 <strong>Tip:</strong> Phrase your scope in business terms (“Our SaaS platform hosted in AWS Sydney serving Australian healthcare providers”)—not just IT systems.
</blockquote>

<hr style="margin:36px 0;border:0;border-top:1px solid #e5e7eb" />

<h2 style="color:#111827;font-size:1.6rem;font-weight:800;margin-top:2rem">2️⃣ Choose a Risk Methodology That Works</h2>

<p>Don’t chase academic perfection. A simple <strong>likelihood × impact matrix</strong> or <strong>heatmap</strong> is enough at first. What matters is that everyone understands and applies it consistently.</p>

<div style="background:#ecfdf5;border-left:4px solid #10b981;padding:12px 18px;border-radius:8px;margin-top:12px">
  ✅ <strong>Outcome:</strong> A shared language for decision-making—linking security priorities to business risk appetite.
</div>

<hr style="margin:36px 0;border:0;border-top:1px solid #e5e7eb" />

<h2 style="color:#111827;font-size:1.6rem;font-weight:800;margin-top:2rem">3️⃣ Build a Living Risk Register</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Start with your <strong>top 5 security risks</strong> today—no massive spreadsheet needed.</li>
  <li>For each, capture: description, owner, likelihood, impact, and current mitigation.</li>
  <li>Update quarterly to show a functioning risk cycle.</li>
</ul>

<blockquote style="background:#fef3c7;border-left:4px solid #f59e0b;padding:12px 18px;border-radius:8px">
  ⚙️ <strong>Pro Tip:</strong> Store your register in SharePoint or Confluence—versioned, owned, and reviewable. That’s instant audit evidence.
</blockquote>

<hr style="margin:36px 0;border:0;border-top:1px solid #e5e7eb" />

<hr style="margin:36px 0;border:0;border-top:1px solid #e5e7eb" />

<h2 style="color:#111827;font-size:1.6rem;font-weight:800;margin-top:2rem">5️⃣ Book Your First Internal Audit</h2>

<p>Even if informal, an internal audit creates momentum. It exposes blind spots, builds familiarity with audit language, and demonstrates leadership commitment.</p>

<blockquote style="background:#eef2ff;border-left:4px solid #4f46e5;padding:12px 18px;border-radius:8px">
  🧭 <strong>Goal:</strong> Treat your first audit as a rehearsal—practice how evidence is presented, who speaks to which controls, and where your ISMS still feels manual.
</blockquote>

<hr style="margin:36px 0;border:0;border-top:1px solid #e5e7eb" />

<h2 style="color:#111827;font-size:1.6rem;font-weight:800;margin-top:2rem">Why These Steps Matter</h2>

<p>Each action creates measurable progress, confidence, and evidence:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Scope clarity</strong> — proves organisational boundaries.</li>
  <li><strong>Risk methodology</strong> — shows decision structure.</li>
  <li><strong>Register updates</strong> — demonstrates continuous management.</li>
  <li><strong>Control mapping</strong> — connects business reality to Annex A.</li>
  <li><strong>Internal audit</strong> — validates everything in practice.</li>
</ul>

<div style="background:#ecfdf5;border-left:4px solid #10b981;padding:14px 20px;border-radius:10px;margin-top:16px">
  🎯 <strong>Bottom line:</strong> You don’t need perfection to start ISO 27001—you just need progress that’s traceable, reviewable, and repeatable.
</div>
