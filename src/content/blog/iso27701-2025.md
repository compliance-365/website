The 2025 revision of **ISO/IEC 27701** strengthens the relationship between privacy and security by aligning more closely with **ISO/IEC 27001:2022**.  
It allows organisations to manage both information security and privacy risks under a unified management system.

## What is ISO/IEC 27701?
ISO/IEC 27701 defines requirements for a **Privacy Information Management System (PIMS)**.  
It extends ISO/IEC 27001 and 27002 to cover how organisations protect and process **personally identifiable information (PII)** as controllers or processors.

The **2025 edition** includes refined guidance for accountability, controller/processor roles, and mappings to global privacy regulations.

---

## What’s new in ISO/IEC 27701:2025
- ✅ Updated control mappings aligned to **ISO/IEC 27002:2022**
- ✅ Stronger guidance on **governance, transparency and oversight**
- ✅ Revised definitions of **controller** and **processor** responsibilities
- ✅ Enhanced interoperability with **GDPR**, **APPs**, and **APRA CPG 235**
- ✅ Clarified expectations for evidence, metrics, and continual improvement

## Benefits of alignment
- 💡 Strengthens privacy & data protection using existing ISMS processes  
- 💡 Streamlines audits and certification by reusing ISO 27001 controls  
- 💡 Demonstrates accountability to regulators and partners  
- 💡 Builds customer trust and transparency  
- 💡 Complements **ISO/IEC 42001** for AI and data ethics governance  

---

## Key takeaways

- **One system, two outcomes:** Aligning ISO/IEC 27701:2025 with your ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS lets you manage security and privacy in a single, auditable framework.  
- **Less audit fatigue:** Reuse governance, risk and control evidence once — to satisfy both security and privacy reviewers.  
- **Trust at the speed of sales:** Clear, repeatable privacy assurance (DPIAs, ROPA, rights handling) mapped to ISO controls speeds enterprise reviews.
