<h2 style="color:#111827;font-size:1.6rem;font-weight:800;margin-top:2rem">1️⃣ Fear: Long, Unpredictable Timelines That Stall Deals</h2>

<p>Traditional compliance projects take 6–18 months — or longer. Deals sit in procurement limbo while you chase documents, consultants, and new tools.</p>

<p>Buyers lose patience. Competitors with faster proof win.</p>

<blockquote style="background:#ecfdf5;border-left:4px solid #10b981;padding:12px 18px;border-radius:8px;margin-top:10px">
  ✅ <strong>Reality:</strong> You can be audit-ready in weeks — not months — when you build inside your existing environment and focus only on what matters.
</blockquote>

<p><strong>How we fix it:</strong> We map the shortest path using your current Microsoft 365 stack — no new licenses, no forced changes. First-time certification or surveillance passed in 8–14 weeks on average.</p>

<hr style="margin:32px 0;border:0;border-top:1px solid #e5e7eb" />

<h2 style="color:#111827;font-size:1.6rem;font-weight:800;margin-top:2rem">2️⃣ Fear: Heavy Engineering Disruption That Kills Velocity</h2>

<p>New tools mean new workflows, new vendors, new training. Engineering gets pulled in for months — product velocity tanks, morale drops.</p>

<p>Founders and CTOs tell us: “We can’t afford to slow down building the product just to check a compliance box.”</p>

<blockquote style="background:#eef2ff;border-left:4px solid #4f46e5;padding:12px 18px;border-radius:8px">
  💡 <strong>Reality:</strong> 100% of our work happens inside your existing environment — no new tools, no forced change management, no endless meetings.
</blockquote>

<p><strong>How we fix it:</strong> We automate evidence collection with the tools you already own (Entra, Defender, Intune, Purview, SharePoint). Your team stays focused on building product, not chasing screenshots.</p>

<hr style="margin:32px 0;border:0;border-top:1px solid #e5e7eb" />

<h2 style="color:#111827;font-size:1.6rem;font-weight:800;margin-top:2rem">3️⃣ Fear: Lack of Credible, Defensible Proof When Procurement & Auditors Ask</h2>

<p>Procurement flags you as “high risk” without evidence. Auditors want screenshots, logs, approvals — but you’re scrambling every time.</p>

<p>Buyers lose confidence. Deals stall or die.</p>

<blockquote style="background:#fef3c7;border-left:4px solid #f59e0b;padding:12px 18px;border-radius:8px">
  ⚠️ <strong>Reality:</strong> Procurement and auditors don’t want perfection — they want defensible, repeatable proof that controls actually work.
</blockquote>

<p><strong>How we fix it:</strong> We build a single, reusable evidence hub in SharePoint — automated exports, version history, clear ownership. When asked, you send one link — clean, auditor-ready, and trusted.</p>
