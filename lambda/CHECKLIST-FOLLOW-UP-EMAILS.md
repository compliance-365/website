# Checklist follow-up emails (drafts)

For people who tick "Email me a short follow-up on my results" on a readiness checklist. In HubSpot they have **Asked for follow-up emails on their checklist results = Yes**, plus **Last website form** (which checklist) and **Last readiness checklist score (%)**.

Three emails, at most, as the checkbox promises. Each one names who it's from and ends with an unsubscribe line (Spam Act 2003). Words in [brackets] are filled in per contact; the HubSpot tokens are noted beside them.
