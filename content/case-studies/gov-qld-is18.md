## Problem
A Queensland Government agency required a clear, evidence-based approach to meet IS18 reporting obligations. Their existing documentation reflected mixed maturity levels across ISO 27001 and the ASD Essential Eight, making it difficult to present a unified compliance view for annual assurance.

## Approach
- Mapped IS18 control requirements to ISO 27001:2022 Annex A and the Essential Eight maturity model  
- Conducted interviews and evidence reviews to establish control maturity baselines  
- Created a single consolidated IS18 assessment report with risk and remediation priorities  
- Developed a dashboard in Microsoft 365 / SharePoint to automate progress tracking  

## Outcome
- Delivered an IS18-aligned report combining ISO 27001 and Essential Eight results  
- Provided a clear, prioritised roadmap for uplift to Maturity Level 3  
- Reduced annual reporting time by more than 50% through automation and evidence reuse  
- Strengthened assurance posture across governance, risk, and technical domains
